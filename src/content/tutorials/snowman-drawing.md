steps:
  - title: Place the main construction shapes
    body: >-
      Sketch the first structure with pale lines: three stacked snowballs,
      carrot nose, scarf, hat, and stick arms. Keep the marks loose and look at
      the whole page rather than one detail. This is the only place where the
      full snowman drawing phrase needs attention; after that, the drawing can
      grow from landmarks. Leave enough margin around the stacked round body
      with cozy accessories so later refinements do not feel cramped.
    tip: Use the side of the pencil for soft construction lines.
  - title: Block in the snowballs
    body: >-
      Add the snowballs using simple curves that follow the first shape. Compare
      their size to the main body before adding detail. If the spacing feels
      uneven, redraw the guide rather than forcing the final outline. Lightly
      mark where the carrot nose will sit so the parts relate to each other and
      the silhouette stays readable.
    tip: Check the largest spacing before erasing any guide lines.
  - title: Set the carrot nose and coal buttons
    body: >-
      Place the carrot nose next, then attach the coal buttons with a clean
      overlap. Watch for tangents where two edges only touch; a small overlap
      usually looks more natural. Keep the new lines lighter than the main
      contour. The goal is to show how the features connect to the form, not to
      finish every small texture mark yet.
    tip: Overlap forms clearly so each part feels attached.
  - title: Refine the outside contour
    body: >-
      Trace around the outer edge slowly and turn the basic shapes into a more
      specific contour. Use longer strokes on calm areas and shorter strokes
      where the form changes direction. Adjust the stacked round body with cozy
      accessories before adding texture. If one side feels too heavy, compare
      the empty space around it and shave the line back with light erasing.
    tip: Darken only the contour you are sure about.
  - title: Add subject details
    body: >-
      Work on soft snow edges and scarf folds with small marks that follow the
      surface. Keep the details grouped instead of spreading identical marks
      everywhere. Add a few accents near the focal area, then leave quieter
      spaces so the drawing can breathe. The scarf and hat should support the
      structure rather than distract from the main shape.
    tip: Cluster detail near the focal point and simplify the edges.
  - title: Clean the guide lines
    body: >-
      Erase construction lines that cut through finished features, especially
      around the snowballs and coal buttons. Do not scrub the paper; lift
      graphite slowly and redraw any softened edges afterward. This cleanup
      stage is also a good time to correct small proportion issues. Step back
      from the page and check whether the subject still reads clearly at a
      glance.
    tip: Use a kneaded eraser if the paper surface is delicate.
  - title: Add light shading and finish
    body: >-
      Choose one light direction and place gentle shadows where forms overlap or
      turn away. Add a cast shadow only if it helps ground the snowman in the
      winter character sketch. For this snowman drawing, keep highlights open
      and avoid covering the whole sketch with gray. Finish by strengthening the
      most important contour lines and softening any leftover construction
      marks.
    tip: One consistent light source is better than many scattered shadows.
