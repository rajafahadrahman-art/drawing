steps:
  - title: Place the main construction shapes
    body: >-
      Sketch the first structure with pale lines: low body box, measured
      wheelbase, arched roofline, and two even tires. Keep the marks loose and
      look at the whole page rather than one detail. This is the only place
      where the full car drawing phrase needs attention; after that, the drawing
      can grow from landmarks. Leave enough margin around the long body with
      balanced wheels and roof so later refinements do not feel cramped.
    tip: Use the side of the pencil for soft construction lines.
  - title: Block in the wheelbase
    body: >-
      Add the wheelbase using simple curves that follow the first shape. Compare
      their size to the main body before adding detail. If the spacing feels
      uneven, redraw the guide rather than forcing the final outline. Lightly
      mark where the tires will sit so the parts relate to each other and the
      silhouette stays readable.
    tip: Check the largest spacing before erasing any guide lines.
  - title: Set the tires and windshield
    body: >-
      Place the tires next, then attach the windshield with a clean overlap.
      Watch for tangents where two edges only touch; a small overlap usually
      looks more natural. Keep the new lines lighter than the main contour. The
      goal is to show how the features connect to the form, not to finish every
      small texture mark yet.
    tip: Overlap forms clearly so each part feels attached.
  - title: Refine the outside contour
    body: >-
      Trace around the outer edge slowly and turn the basic shapes into a more
      specific contour. Use longer strokes on calm areas and shorter strokes
      where the form changes direction. Adjust the long body with balanced
      wheels and roof before adding texture. If one side feels too heavy,
      compare the empty space around it and shave the line back with light
      erasing.
    tip: Darken only the contour you are sure about.
  - title: Add subject details
    body: >-
      Work on tire rims and window reflections with small marks that follow the
      surface. Keep the details grouped instead of spreading identical marks
      everywhere. Add a few accents near the focal area, then leave quieter
      spaces so the drawing can breathe. The hood and roofline should support
      the structure rather than distract from the main shape.
    tip: Cluster detail near the focal point and simplify the edges.
  - title: Clean the guide lines
    body: >-
      Erase construction lines that cut through finished features, especially
      around the wheelbase and windshield. Do not scrub the paper; lift graphite
      slowly and redraw any softened edges afterward. This cleanup stage is also
      a good time to correct small proportion issues. Step back from the page
      and check whether the subject still reads clearly at a glance.
    tip: Use a kneaded eraser if the paper surface is delicate.
  - title: Add light shading and finish
    body: >-
      Choose one light direction and place gentle shadows where forms overlap or
      turn away. Add a cast shadow only if it helps ground the car in the
      side-view vehicle sketch. For this car drawing, keep highlights open and
      avoid covering the whole sketch with gray. Finish by strengthening the
      most important contour lines and softening any leftover construction
      marks.
    tip: One consistent light source is better than many scattered shadows.
  - title: Build richer bumper texture
    body: >-
      For a fuller version, add secondary texture around the bumper and
      headlights. Let the marks change length and pressure so they do not form a
      repeated pattern. Follow the direction of the form: curve strokes around
      round areas and angle them along flat planes. Stop before the texture
      covers the drawing's clean construction.
    tip: Vary stroke length to avoid a stamped texture.
  - title: Strengthen depth and overlap
    body: >-
      Deepen the small shadows under overlapping parts and near the base of the
      subject. The headlights should sit behind or in front of nearby shapes
      with clear edge priority. Use slightly darker line weight where one form
      crosses another. These contrast choices make the drawing feel layered
      without adding unnecessary background detail.
    tip: Darker overlap lines create depth quickly.
  - title: Review proportions before final lines
    body: >-
      Before the final pass, compare the main height, width, and angle of the
      sketch. Look at the door seam in relation to the full silhouette and
      correct any drift with pale marks. A stronger car drawing comes from these
      quiet checks. Once the structure feels balanced, redraw the final contour
      with confident pressure and keep interior lines lighter.
    tip: A final proportion check prevents heavy corrections later.
